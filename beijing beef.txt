cut beef against grain

marinate with: 
1 tbsp shoyu 
1 tbsp sake
1/2 tsp baking soda
a little water
1/2 cup cornstarch
white pepper 
salt

chop half onion into square
chop bell pepper into square
mince 4 clove of garlic

Sauce:
2 tbsp shoyu 
3 tbsp hoisin
2 tbsp oyster sauce
2 tbsp ketchup
a little sriracha
2 tbsp vinegar
3 tbsp brown sugar

shallow fry beef
take out and then stir fry the onion and bell pepper
once it's fragrant pour in sauce
once reduced, add in meat
