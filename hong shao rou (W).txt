cut pork belly into cubes

boil for 10 minutes and then strain

In pan on medium heat, add 1/6 cup water and 2 tbsp brown sugar
cook until it starts to caramelize

add in pork belly 2 slices ginger and 2 cloves garlic
stir 2 minutes

add 1.5 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp dark soy sauce, 1/2 packet lu bao, 1.5 cups water	

cover with lid and simmer for 1.5 hour

once tender, remove lid, raise heat, and let sauce thicken for 5-10 min
