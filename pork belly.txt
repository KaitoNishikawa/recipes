sauce:
2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
1 tsp 5 spice powder
1 tsp salt
1 tsp sugar
1/2 tsp white pepper powder

score bottom of pork belly in cube
run in sauce

dry top of pork belly and poke
season skin with generous amount of salt 

wrap in aluminum foil
dry top again
air fry 250F for 40-50 min

pat dry again
coat skin with oil
375F for 20 min
