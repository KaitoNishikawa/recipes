cut beef and boil in water
アクを取って

in pot boil oil and 2 tbsp sugar on low heat (don't stir)
add beef when caramelized
add in ginger and green onion and chili pepper

turn up heat and stir fry for a minute
add a splash of Shaoxing wine and cook until evaporate
add 2 tbsp 豆瓣酱 and cook for a minute
add 2 tbsp soy sauce 1 tbsp dark soy sauce 1 tbsp oyster sauce and black pepper

add 2 cups HOT water to beef
add 2 spice packet

simmer for 1 hour
add salt if you want
